# Beyond Components — Management Presentation

## Purpose

A 15-slide executive discussion for Design, Engineering, and Product Leadership on why design systems need to standardize design knowledge as well as reusable implementation.

## Audience

- Head of Design
- Design Leads
- Engineering Leads
- Product Leadership

## Format

- Duration: 20–30 minutes
- Style: executive, architectural, and evidence-based
- Structure: one key message and one suggested visual per slide
- Source: [Beyond Components whitepaper](../../whitepapers/beyond-components/README.md)

## Files

- [Complete slide deck](slides.md)
- [Speaker notes](speaker-notes.md)
- [Mermaid diagram assets](assets/diagrams/)

## Narrative

The deck moves from strategic pressure, through the limits of current design-system architecture, to a knowledge platform direction, a bounded UI Foundations example, and leadership decisions about relevance, assumptions, pilots, and success.
