# Gestalt Principles

## Question

How do humans perceive structure?

## Purpose

Gestalt principles describe how people perceive relationships before they read details.

## Principles

- Proximity: nearby elements are perceived as related.
- Similarity: elements with shared appearance are perceived as belonging together.
- Continuity: aligned or flowing elements are perceived as part of a continuous structure.
- Closure: people complete incomplete shapes or patterns when enough cues exist.
- Figure and ground: people separate focal content from its surrounding field.
- Common region: elements inside the same boundary are perceived as a group.
- Connectedness: visually connected elements are perceived as strongly related.
- Prägnanz: people tend to perceive the simplest stable organization available.

## Guidance

Use perceptual grouping to make relationships obvious before explanation is needed. Avoid visual cues that imply relationships the interface does not actually support.
