# Information Architecture

## Question

How should information be organized?

## Purpose

Information architecture gives content, navigation, and tasks a structure that matches how people understand the domain.

## Principles

- Orientation: people should know where they are, what is available, and what can happen next.
- Grouping: related items should be grouped by meaning, task, or decision point.
- Findability: important paths and information should be easy to locate without hidden knowledge.
- Recognition: navigation and labels should use terms people can recognize.
- Progressive disclosure: reveal complexity when it becomes relevant.
- Consistent structure: similar areas should organize information in similar ways.
- Clear boundaries: sections, categories, and states should not blur into each other.
- Minimal memory burden: people should not have to remember prior screens or hidden rules to proceed.

## Guidance

Organize information around user goals and domain meaning, not internal ownership or implementation structure.
